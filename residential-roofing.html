Residential roofing pricing in Texas

$180 - $450 per square depending on scope

Pricing for residential roofing services in Texas typically ranges from $180 to $450 per square, depending on material choices and roof complexity. Factors like roof size, pitch, and the extent of repairs needed can influence the final cost. Our team provides transparent pricing and free estimates to help you understand the investment required for your roofing project.

Seasonal Considerations for Roofing in Donna

Donna's climate affects roofing needs throughout the year. Understanding seasonal changes can help homeowners prepare.

Spring

Spring brings heavy rains, which can lead to roof leaks if not properly maintained.

Tip: Schedule a roof inspection to identify potential issues before the rainy season.

Summer

Summer heat can cause roofing materials to expand and contract, leading to potential damage.

Tip: Ensure proper ventilation to reduce heat buildup and prolong roof life.

Fall

Fall is a time for falling leaves, which can clog gutters and lead to water damage.

Tip: Clean gutters regularly to prevent water backup and roof damage.

Winter

Winter can bring ice storms, which may cause ice damming on roofs.

Tip: Consider installing heat cables to prevent ice buildup and protect your roof.

Signs You Need Roofing in Corpus Christi

Recognizing when you need roofing services in Corpus Christi is crucial for maintaining your home's integrity. Look for these signs to determine if you need assistance with your roof.

  • Visible leaks or water stains

    If you notice leaks or water stains on your ceiling, it indicates a potential roof issue that needs immediate attention.

  • Missing or damaged shingles

    Missing or cracked shingles can lead to further damage and should be repaired or replaced promptly.

  • Granules in gutters

    Finding shingle granules in your gutters is a sign that your roof is deteriorating and may need replacement.

  • Sagging roofline

    A sagging roofline can indicate structural issues and should be evaluated by a professional immediately.

  • Increased energy bills

    If your energy bills are rising, it may be due to poor insulation or damage in your roof allowing heat to escape.

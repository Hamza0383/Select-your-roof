Granbury through the year

How Seasons Affect Roofing in Granbury

Granbury's climate significantly impacts roofing demands throughout the year. Each season presents unique challenges and opportunities for homeowners to maintain their roofs effectively. Here's how the changing seasons can affect your roofing needs.

Spring

Spring often brings heavy rains, which can lead to leaks if your roof is not in good condition. Regular inspections are essential.

Tip: Schedule a roof inspection in early spring to ensure your roof can handle the upcoming rain.

Summer

Summer heat can cause roofing materials to expand and contract, potentially leading to cracks or warping.

Tip: Consider routine maintenance checks during summer to address any wear and tear before the fall storms.

Fall

Fall brings falling leaves that can clog gutters and lead to water pooling on the roof, increasing the risk of leaks.

Tip: Clean your gutters regularly in the fall to prevent water damage and ensure proper drainage.

Winter

Winter can bring ice and snow, which may lead to ice damming and increased weight on your roof.

Tip: Ensure your roof is well-insulated and ventilated to minimize the risk of ice dams this winter.

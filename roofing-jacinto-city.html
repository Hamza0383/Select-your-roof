Mold growth from humidity

Our approach: We ensure proper ventilation and use mold-resistant materials to combat this problem.

04

Ice damming in winter

Why it happens: Ice damming occurs when snow melts and refreezes at the eaves, causing water to back up under shingles.

Our approach: We recommend proper insulation and ventilation to reduce the risk of ice damming during winter months.

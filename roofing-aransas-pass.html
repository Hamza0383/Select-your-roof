How Seasons Affect Roofing in Aransas Pass

The climate in Aransas Pass influences roofing needs throughout the year. Understanding seasonal patterns can help homeowners prepare for potential issues. Here's what to expect in each season.

Spring

Spring brings heavy rains that can expose leaks and other roofing issues. Regular inspections are essential.

Tip: Schedule an inspection to ensure your roof can handle the spring rains and address any potential leaks.

Summer

Summer heat can cause roofing materials to expand, leading to cracks and wear.

Tip: Consider reflective roofing materials to reduce heat absorption and prolong roof life.

Fall

Fall often brings storms that can damage roofs, especially from high winds and falling debris.

Tip: Inspect your roof for loose shingles and debris buildup before the winter season.

Winter

Winter can introduce ice damming issues on roofs, particularly in shaded areas.

Tip: Ensure proper insulation and ventilation to prevent ice damming and protect your roof.

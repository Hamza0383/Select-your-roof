Fairview through the year

How Seasons Affect Roofing in Fairview

Fairview's climate influences roofing needs throughout the year. Understanding seasonal changes can help homeowners prepare for potential issues.

Spring

Spring brings heavy rains, which can lead to leaks if roofs are not properly maintained. Inspecting for damage is crucial.

Tip: Schedule a roof inspection in early spring to address any winter damage before the rainy season.

Summer

Summer heat can cause roofing materials to expand and contract, leading to potential cracks or damage.

Tip: Ensure proper ventilation in your attic to help regulate temperature and prolong your roof's lifespan.

Fall

Fall often brings strong winds that can dislodge shingles and cause debris buildup on roofs.

Tip: Clean gutters and downspouts in the fall to prevent clogs that can lead to water pooling on your roof.

Winter

Winter can bring snow and ice, leading to ice damming and potential leaks.

Tip: Consider adding insulation to your attic to prevent heat loss and reduce the risk of ice dams forming.

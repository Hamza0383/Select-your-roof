How Seasons Affect Roofing in Marshall

Marshall's climate influences roofing demands throughout the year. Different seasons bring unique challenges and maintenance needs for homeowners.

Spring

Spring often brings heavy rains, which can expose leaks in older roofs. Regular inspections are essential to identify issues early.

Tip: Schedule a roof inspection in early spring to ensure your roof is ready for the rainy season.

Summer

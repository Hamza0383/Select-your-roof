How Seasons Affect Roofing in Andrews

The climate in Andrews significantly influences roofing demand throughout the year. Homeowners should be aware of seasonal challenges to maintain their roofs effectively.

Spring

Spring often brings heavy rains, increasing the risk of leaks and water damage.

Tip: Schedule a roof inspection to ensure your roof is ready for the rainy season.

Summer

Summer heat can cause shingles to warp or crack, leading to potential leaks.

Tip: Consider roof maintenance to check for any signs of wear and tear.

Fall

Fall brings falling leaves, which can clog gutters and lead to water pooling on roofs.

Tip: Regularly clean gutters to prevent water damage during the rainy season.

Winter

Winter can bring ice and snow, leading to ice damming and additional weight on roofs.

Tip: Ensure proper insulation and ventilation to minimize ice damming risks.

Common Roofing Problems in Socorro

Socorro's unique climate and building styles contribute to specific roofing issues. Understanding these common problems can help homeowners take proactive measures.

01

Frequent roof leaks after storms

Why it happens: Heavy rains can overwhelm roofing systems, especially older ones, leading to leaks and water damage.

Our approach: We conduct thorough inspections after storms to identify and repair any leaks quickly.

02

Hail damage to roofing materials

Why it happens: Hail can cause dents and punctures, especially in softer materials like asphalt shingles.

Our approach: We specialize in hail damage assessments and use durable materials to withstand future storms.

03

Poor ventilation causing heat buildup

Why it happens: Inadequate ventilation can lead to heat buildup in attics, causing shingles to deteriorate faster.

Our approach: We recommend and install proper ventilation systems to extend the lifespan of your roof.

04

Ice damming in winter months

Why it happens: Ice dams occur when melting snow refreezes at the roof's edge, causing water to back up under shingles.

Our approach: We offer solutions to improve insulation and ventilation, preventing ice dam formation.

Socorro through the year

How Seasons Affect Roofing in Socorro

Socorro's climate influences roofing maintenance and repair needs throughout the year. Understanding these seasonal factors is crucial for homeowners.

Spring

Spring often brings heavy rains that can expose roofing issues. Regular inspections are recommended to prevent leaks.

Tip: Schedule a roof inspection after winter to address any damage before the rainy season.

Summer

Hot temperatures can lead to shingle deterioration and heat buildup in attics.

Tip: Consider installing reflective roofing materials to reduce cooling costs during the summer months.

Fall

Fall brings falling leaves that can clog gutters and cause water to back up on roofs.

Tip: Clean gutters regularly to ensure proper drainage and prevent water damage.

Winter

Winter can bring snow and ice, leading to potential ice damming and roof leaks.

Tip: Ensure your roof is properly insulated to prevent heat loss and ice dam formation.

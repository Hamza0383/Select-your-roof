Graham through the year

Seasonal Roofing Considerations in Graham

Graham's climate affects roofing needs throughout the year. Understanding seasonal changes can help homeowners prepare for potential issues.

Spring

Spring often brings heavy rains and hail, which can lead to roof leaks and damage.

Tip: Schedule a roof inspection in early spring to address any damage before the rainy season.

Summer

Hot temperatures can cause roofing materials to expand and contract, leading to potential damage.

Tip: Ensure your roof is well-ventilated to prevent overheating and damage during the summer months.

Fall

Fall brings falling leaves that can clog gutters and lead to water pooling on roofs.

Tip: Schedule a gutter cleaning and roof inspection in the fall to prepare for winter.

Winter

Cold temperatures can lead to ice damming and snow accumulation on roofs, which can cause leaks.

Tip: Regularly clear snow from your roof and ensure proper insulation to prevent ice dams.

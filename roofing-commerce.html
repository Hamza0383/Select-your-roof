How Seasons Affect Roofing in Commerce

The climate in Commerce influences roofing needs throughout the year. Understanding seasonal variations can help you maintain your roof effectively.

Spring

Spring often brings heavy rains that can expose roof leaks and damage.

Tip: Schedule a roof inspection in early spring to address any issues before the rainy season.

Summer

Tip: Clean your gutters regularly to prevent water backup and roof damage during the rainy season.

Winter

Tip: Consider winterizing your roof with proper insulation and ventilation to prevent ice dams.

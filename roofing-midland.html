01

Roof leaks due to aging shingles

Why it happens: As shingles age, they become brittle and lose their ability to protect against water intrusion, leading to leaks.

Our approach: We recommend regular inspections and timely replacements to prevent leaks and extend the roof's lifespan.

02

Storm damage from hail

Why it happens: Hail storms can cause significant damage to roofing materials, especially if they are older or not properly installed.

Our approach: Our team specializes in hail damage repairs and can quickly assess and restore your roof after a storm.

03

Poor insulation leading to energy loss

Why it happens: Inadequate insulation can cause heat loss in winter and heat gain in summer, leading to higher energy bills.

Our approach: We offer solutions that improve insulation and energy efficiency, helping homeowners save on utility costs.

04

Ice damming during winter

Why it happens: Ice dams form when heat escapes from the roof, melting snow that refreezes at the eaves, causing water to back up.

Our approach: We provide insulation and ventilation solutions to prevent ice damming and protect your roof.

Midland through the year

How Seasons Affect Roofing in Midland

Midland experiences distinct seasons that can impact roofing needs. Understanding these seasonal variations is essential for homeowners to maintain their roofs effectively.

Spring

Spring in Midland can bring heavy rains, increasing the risk of leaks and water damage. It's essential to inspect your roof for any potential issues.

Tip: Schedule a roof inspection after winter to address any damage before the rainy season.

Summer

Summer heat can cause roofing materials to expand and contract, leading to cracks and deterioration.

Tip: Consider reflective roofing materials to reduce heat absorption and lower energy costs.

Fall

Fall brings falling leaves, which can clog gutters and lead to water pooling on the roof.

Tip: Clean gutters regularly to prevent water damage and ensure proper drainage.

Winter

Winter can bring snow and ice, increasing the risk of ice dams and roof collapse due to heavy snow loads.

Tip: Ensure your attic is well-insulated and ventilated to prevent ice dam formation.

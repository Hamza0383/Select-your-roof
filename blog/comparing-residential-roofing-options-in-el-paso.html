Comparing residential roofing options in El Paso

Discover the best residential roofing options available in El Paso, Texas, to protect your home effectively.

S
Select Your RoofSeptember 2026 · 4 min read

El Paso homeowners have several residential roofing options to choose from, including asphalt shingles, metal roofing, and tile roofing. Each option has its unique benefits and considerations, making it essential to understand these differences before making a decision.

Residential roofing is a category that encompasses various materials and styles used to cover and protect homes from weather elements. This guide will break down the most common roofing options available in El Paso, helping you make an informed choice.

What are the main residential roofing options?

In El Paso, the most popular residential roofing options include:

  • Asphalt Shingles
  • Metal Roofing
  • Tile Roofing
  • Wood Shingles and Shakes
  • Flat Roofing Systems

Each of these materials offers distinct advantages, and the choice often depends on your budget, aesthetic preferences, and the specific requirements of your home.

How do asphalt shingles perform in El Paso?

Asphalt shingles are the most commonly used roofing material in the United States, including El Paso. They are favored for their affordability and ease of installation. In 2026, the average cost for asphalt shingles ranges from $90 to $100 per square, including materials and installation.

In El Paso's hot climate, asphalt shingles can last between 15 to 30 years, depending on the quality of the shingles and the installation. They are designed to withstand heat but may require more frequent maintenance compared to other materials. Regular inspections and prompt repairs are crucial to extending their lifespan.

For homeowners considering a roof replacement, the roof replacement services Texas offered by Select Your Roof can help ensure your new shingles are installed correctly.

What are the benefits of metal roofing?

Metal roofing is gaining popularity in El Paso due to its durability and energy efficiency. Metal roofs can last 40 to 70 years, making them a long-term investment. They reflect solar heat, which can significantly reduce cooling costs during the hot summer months.

Moreover, metal roofing is resistant to extreme weather conditions, including heavy winds and hail, which can be a concern in the region. Installation costs for metal roofing typically range from $300 to $700 per square, depending on the type of metal used. For those interested in this option, Select Your Roof provides expert residential roofing services Texas tailored to your needs.

When should you consider tile roofing?

Tile roofing is a traditional choice that offers exceptional durability and aesthetic appeal. It is particularly well-suited for the architectural style of many homes in El Paso. Tile roofs can last over 50 years with proper maintenance, making them one of the most durable roofing options available.

While the initial installation cost can be higher-ranging from $600 to $800 per square-tile roofs can provide excellent long-term value due to their longevity and low maintenance requirements. Homeowners should consider tile roofing if they are looking for a long-lasting, visually appealing option that complements the local architectural styles.

What are common roofing mistakes to avoid?

Homeowners often make several common mistakes when choosing or maintaining their roofs:

  • Ignoring regular inspections and maintenance
  • Choosing roofing materials without considering climate
  • Overlooking ventilation needs, which can lead to moisture problems
  • Not hiring qualified roofing contractors for installation

Avoiding these pitfalls can save you money and extend the life of your roof. If you need assistance, consider contacting Select Your Roof for roof inspection services Texas to ensure your roofing system is in optimal condition.

When to call a professional roofing contractor?

If you notice signs of damage, such as leaks, missing shingles, or sagging areas, it's crucial to call a professional. Delaying repairs can lead to more significant issues and higher costs. Frequently Asked Questions

1. What is the average lifespan of asphalt shingles?
Asphalt shingles typically last between 15 to 30 years, depending on the quality and maintenance.

2. How much does a new roof cost in El Paso?
The cost of a new roof in El Paso varies widely, generally ranging from $5,000 to $15,000 depending on materials and labor.

3. Can I install a new roof over my old one?
In some cases, you can install a new roof over an existing one, but this depends on local building codes and the condition of the existing roof.

4. What are the signs I need a new roof?
Common signs include leaks, missing shingles, and visible sagging. If you notice these, it's time to consult a professional.

5. How often should I have my roof inspected?
It's recommended to have your roof inspected at least once a year, especially after severe weather events.

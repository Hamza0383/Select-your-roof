How to spot hail damage on your Texas roof

Discover how to identify hail damage on your Texas roof and the steps to take for repairs.

S
Select Your RoofSeptember 2026 · 3 min read

Hail damage on your roof can be subtle yet serious. Spotting it early can save you from costly repairs down the line. This guide will help you identify hail damage, understand its implications, and know when to call for professional help.

Hail damage is a type of roofing damage caused by ice pellets falling from the sky during storms. These pellets can vary in size and can lead to significant issues if not addressed promptly.

What is hail damage?

Hail damage occurs when ice pellets strike your roof with enough force to break or displace shingles. This can lead to leaks and further structural issues if not repaired quickly. Understanding how hail works and the potential damage it can cause is crucial, especially in areas like Texas, where hail storms are common.

How can you identify hail damage?

Identifying hail damage involves a careful inspection of your roof. Look for signs such as:

  • Missing or broken shingles
  • Granules from shingles in gutters or on the ground
  • dents on metal roof surfaces or vents
  • Cracks or fractures in shingles

After a hail storm, it's advisable to conduct a thorough inspection or hire a professional to assess the damage. The best roofing contractors for repair in Texas can provide valuable insights into the condition of your roof.

Common signs of hail damage

When inspecting your roof, keep an eye out for these common signs of hail damage:

  1. Shingle Dents: Look for round, dark spots on shingles, which indicate the protective granules have been knocked off.
  2. Granule Accumulation: If you find granules in your gutters, it's a strong indication that your shingles have been compromised.
  3. Cracked Shingles: Hail can cause shingles to crack, leading to potential leaks.
  4. Debris in Yard: Pieces of your roof may end up on the ground after a hail storm, signaling damage.

What to do after spotting hail damage

After identifying hail damage, take these steps:

  • Document the damage with photos for insurance purposes.
  • Contact your insurance company to file a claim.
  • Schedule a professional hail damage roof repair Texas inspection.
  • Consider temporary measures, like tarping, to prevent further water intrusion.

Many homeowners in Texas face similar issues after severe weather. Knowing the steps to take can make the process smoother.

Common mistakes when checking for hail damage

Homeowners often make several mistakes when inspecting for hail damage:

  • Not checking all roof areas, including valleys and edges.
  • Assuming minor damage isn't worth addressing.
  • Failing to document damage properly for insurance claims.
  • Delaying repairs, which can lead to more extensive damage.

When to call a professional

If you notice significant damage or are unsure about the extent of the hail impact, it's time to call in a professional. Select Your Roof offers comprehensive roof inspection services Texas to assess the damage accurately and recommend necessary repairs.

Frequently Asked Questions

1. How can I tell if my roof has hail damage? Look for missing shingles, granule loss, and dents on roofing materials. A thorough inspection is recommended.

2. What should I do if I find hail damage? Document the damage, contact your insurance, and schedule a professional inspection for repair.

3. How much does hail damage roof repair cost? Typical costs for hail damage repair in Texas can range from $1,000 to $3,000, depending on the extent of the damage.

4. Can I repair hail damage myself? While minor repairs may be attempted, it's often best to hire professionals for safety and durability.

5. How long does it take to repair hail damage? Most hail damage repairs can be completed within a few days to a week, depending on the severity and weather conditions.

What to do if your roof has storm damage in Dallas

Discover the essential steps to take if your roof has storm damage in Dallas, including how to assess and repair it effectively.

S
Select Your RoofSeptember 2026 · 3 min read

Storm damage can significantly impact the integrity of your roof. If your roof has storm damage in Dallas, you should first assess the situation and take appropriate action to prevent further damage.

This article will guide you through identifying storm damage, immediate actions to take, how to assess the damage, and when to call a professional.

What is storm damage?

Storm damage refers to the harm caused to roofs and other structures due to severe weather conditions, such as high winds, hail, and heavy rain. Understanding the types of storm damage can help you take timely action.

How to identify storm damage on your roof

Identifying storm damage on your roof requires a careful inspection. Look for missing shingles, dents in the roofing material, or visible leaks inside your home. Common signs of damage include:

  • Missing or broken shingles
  • Dents or punctures in metal roofing
  • Granules from asphalt shingles in gutters
  • Water stains on ceilings or walls

If you notice any of these signs, it's crucial to take immediate action to prevent further issues.

What to do immediately after a storm

After a storm, your first step should be to ensure safety. Avoid climbing onto the roof unless you are trained and equipped. Instead, check your property for visible damage from the ground. If you suspect significant damage, contact a professional roofing service like roof repair services Texas for assistance.

How to assess the extent of the damage

To assess the extent of storm damage, consider the following steps:

  1. Conduct a visual inspection from the ground.
  2. Use binoculars to inspect hard-to-reach areas.
  3. Look for signs of water intrusion inside your home.
  4. Document any damage with photos for insurance claims.

If you find significant damage, it may be necessary to schedule a professional roof inspection services Texas to evaluate the situation thoroughly.

When to call a professional

If you observe extensive damage or if your roof is leaking, it's time to call a professional. Experts can provide the best solutions for your specific needs, whether it's storm damage roof repair Texas or a complete roof replacement.

Frequently Asked Questions

1. How much does it cost to repair storm damage in Dallas? Repair costs can range from $200 to $1,500 depending on the extent of the damage and materials needed.

2. How long does roof repair take? Most roof repairs can be completed in one to three days, depending on the severity of the damage and weather conditions.

3. Can I file an insurance claim for storm damage? Yes, many homeowners insurance policies cover storm damage. Contact your insurance agent for specific guidelines.

4. What should I do if my roof is leaking after a storm? If your roof is leaking, temporarily cover the area with a tarp to prevent further damage and contact a roofing professional immediately.

5. How can I prevent storm damage in the future? Regular roof maintenance, inspections, and timely repairs can help prevent storm damage. Consider scheduling a roof maintenance services Texas to keep your roof in optimal condition.

Common signs you need a roof inspection in Austin

Discover the key signs that indicate you need a roof inspection in Austin to maintain your home's integrity and safety.

S
Select Your RoofSeptember 2026 · 3 min read

A roof inspection is essential for maintaining the integrity of your home. If you notice any of the signs below, it's time to schedule a roof inspection in Austin.

In this article, we will explore the common signs that indicate your roof may need professional attention, the importance of regular inspections, and when to call Select Your Roof for help.

What is a roof inspection?

A roof inspection is a thorough examination of your roof's condition, typically conducted by a qualified roofing contractor. This process identifies any potential issues, such as leaks, damage, or wear that could lead to more significant problems if left unaddressed.

Why are roof inspections important?

Roof inspections are crucial for several reasons. They help you identify minor issues before they escalate into costly repairs. Regular inspections can extend the lifespan of your roof, ensuring that it remains a protective barrier against the elements. In Austin, where weather conditions can vary, having a reliable roof is essential for your home's safety.

Common signs you need a roof inspection

Several indicators suggest it's time for a roof inspection in Austin. Here are the most common signs:

  • Missing or Damaged Shingles: If you notice any missing, cracked, or curling shingles, it's time to call for a roof inspection.
  • Water Stains on Ceilings: Water stains inside your home can indicate a leak in your roof that requires immediate attention.
  • Moss or Algae Growth: While some growth may seem harmless, it can trap moisture and lead to roof damage over time.
  • Granules in Gutters: If you find shingle granules in your gutters, it may indicate that your shingles are deteriorating.
  • Age of the Roof: If your roof is over 20 years old, it's wise to schedule an inspection to assess its condition.

What to watch out for

When inspecting your roof, keep an eye out for the following issues:

  • Flashing Damage: Damaged flashing around chimneys, vents, and skylights can lead to leaks.
  • Pooling Water: Water that collects on your roof can cause damage over time, especially if it doesn't drain properly.
  • Signs of Pests: Rodents or insects can compromise your roof's integrity, so look for signs of infestation.

When to call a professional

If you notice any of the signs mentioned above, it's time to reach out to a professional for a roof inspection. Frequently Asked Questions

  • How often should I have my roof inspected? It's recommended to have your roof inspected at least once a year, especially after severe weather events.
  • What does a roof inspection cost? In 2026, the typical cost for a roof inspection in Austin ranges from $150 to $400, depending on the size and condition of your roof.
  • Can I inspect my roof myself? While you can perform a visual inspection, hiring a professional ensures a thorough evaluation and identification of hidden issues.
  • What happens if I ignore roof problems? Ignoring roof issues can lead to significant damage, requiring costly repairs or even a full roof replacement.
  • How long does a roof inspection take? A standard roof inspection typically takes about 1 to 2 hours, depending on the roof size and complexity.

How to prepare your roof for the Texas hurricane season

Prepare your roof for the Texas hurricane season with these essential tips and expert advice from Select Your Roof.

S
Select Your RoofSeptember 2026 · 3 min read

Preparing your roof for the Texas hurricane season is essential to protect your home from severe weather damage. Proper preparation can prevent costly repairs and ensure your roof withstands high winds and heavy rains. This article covers crucial steps to inspect, repair, and maintain your roof before hurricane season hits.

Roof preparation for hurricane season is the process of assessing and reinforcing your roof's structural integrity to withstand intense storms. In Texas, hurricane season typically runs from June to November, making it vital to address any roofing issues well before the storms arrive.

Why is roof preparation important for hurricanes?

Roof preparation is critical because hurricanes can produce winds exceeding 150 mph, which can easily damage or lift off poorly maintained roofs. In Texas, homes along the Gulf Coast are particularly vulnerable to hurricane damage, making it essential to ensure your roof is in optimal condition.

How to inspect your roof before hurricane season

Conducting a thorough roof inspection is the first step in preparing for hurricane season. Start by checking for visible damage, such as missing shingles, cracks, or sagging areas. Look for signs of wear around flashing and vents, as these areas are often susceptible to leaks.

  • Check the condition of shingles: Look for curling, cracking, or missing shingles.
  • Inspect flashing: Ensure it is securely attached and free from rust or corrosion.
  • Examine gutters and downspouts: Clear any debris to ensure proper drainage.

If you notice significant damage, consider scheduling a professional roof inspection services Texas to identify hidden issues and provide recommendations.

What repairs should be made prior to hurricanes?

Addressing repairs before hurricane season can prevent more severe damage later. Common repairs include:

  • Replacing missing or damaged shingles to prevent leaks.
  • Securing loose flashing and seals around vents and chimneys.
  • Reinforcing the roof structure if sagging or weak points are found.

In some cases, you may need to consider a roof replacement services Texas if your roof is nearing the end of its lifespan or has extensive damage.

How to maintain your roof during hurricane season

Regular maintenance during hurricane season is essential for ensuring your roof remains intact. After each storm, check for any new damage and address issues immediately. Keep gutters clear to avoid water pooling on your roof, which can lead to leaks.

Additionally, consider trimming any overhanging branches that could fall during high winds, as this can help prevent damage to your roof.

Common mistakes to avoid when preparing your roof

Many homeowners make mistakes that can lead to costly repairs or dangerous situations. Avoid these common pitfalls:

  • Neglecting regular inspections: Skipping inspections can lead to unnoticed damage.
  • Ignoring minor repairs: Small issues can escalate into major problems.
  • Waiting until the last minute: Start preparations well before hurricane season begins.

When to call a professional for roof preparation

Knowing when to call in a professional is crucial. If you notice extensive damage or are unsure about the condition of your roof, it's best to contact a qualified roofing contractor. Select Your Roof offers expert roof repair services Texas to help you prepare your home for the storms ahead.

Frequently Asked Questions

1. How often should I inspect my roof for hurricane readiness?
Inspect your roof at least twice a year, ideally before and after hurricane season, to ensure it remains in good condition.

2. What are the signs that my roof needs replacement?
Signs include extensive shingle damage, leaks, and a sagging roof structure. If repairs exceed 30% of the roof's value, replacement may be more cost-effective.

3. How much does a roof inspection typically cost?
The average cost for a roof inspection in Texas ranges from $150 to $400, depending on the size and complexity of your roof.

4. Can I prepare my roof for hurricanes myself?
While some tasks can be done by homeowners, such as clearing gutters, significant repairs should be handled by professionals to ensure safety and compliance with local codes.

5. What should I do if my roof is damaged during a hurricane?
After the storm, assess the damage and contact a roofing professional immediately for emergency repairs to prevent further issues.

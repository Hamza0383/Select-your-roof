The cost of roof replacement in McAllen: a complete breakdown

Discover the detailed costs and factors involved in roof replacement in McAllen, Texas, for 2026.

SSelect Your Roof ·September 2026 ·10 min read

Roof replacement in McAllen typically costs between $5,000 and $15,000, depending on various factors such as roofing material and the size of your home. This article provides a comprehensive breakdown of the costs associated with roof replacement in McAllen, Texas, and what homeowners should expect during the process.

Roof replacement is the process of removing an existing roof and installing a new one, which becomes necessary when the roof is damaged beyond repair or has reached the end of its lifespan.

Factors affecting the cost of roof replacement

Several factors influence the cost of roof replacement in McAllen. Understanding these factors can help you budget effectively.

  • Roofing material: The type of material you choose significantly impacts the overall cost. Asphalt shingles are typically the most affordable, while metal and tile roofs can be more expensive.
  • Size of the roof: Larger roofs require more materials and labor, which increases the total cost.
  • Labor costs: Labor rates in McAllen can vary. Skilled labor may charge more, but it often results in better quality work.
  • Removal of old roofing: If your existing roof needs to be removed, this will add to the labor costs.
  • Permits and inspections: Local regulations may require permits, which can add to the overall expense.

Average cost of roof replacement in McAllen

The average cost of roof replacement in McAllen ranges from $5,000 to $15,000 in 2026. However, prices can vary based on the factors mentioned above. Here's a more detailed breakdown:

  • Asphalt shingles: $5,000 - $10,000
  • Metal roofing: $10,000 - $15,000
  • Tile roofing: $12,000 - $20,000

Many homeowners in McAllen also consider the long-term value of different roofing materials. For example, while asphalt shingles are cheaper upfront, metal roofs can last longer and offer better energy efficiency.

When to consider roof replacement

Homeowners should consider roof replacement if they notice several issues:

  • Significant leaks or water damage inside the home.
  • Missing or damaged shingles.
  • Visible sagging in the roof structure.
  • Age of the roof exceeding 20 years for asphalt shingles or 50 years for metal roofs.

If you're unsure about the condition of your roof, a professional roof inspection can provide clarity on whether replacement is necessary.

Common mistakes to avoid

When planning for roof replacement in McAllen, avoid these common mistakes:

  • Ignoring local building codes: Ensure your new roof complies with McAllen's regulations, as failure to do so can result in fines.
  • Choosing the cheapest option: While saving money is important, opting for the lowest bid can lead to poor quality work.
  • Not checking for warranties: Always inquire about warranties for both materials and labor to protect your investment.

When to call a professional

Knowing when to call a professional for roof replacement is crucial. If you notice multiple signs of damage or if your roof is nearing the end of its lifespan, it's time to consult with experts. Select Your Roof offers comprehensive roof replacement services Texas tailored to your needs.

Frequently asked questions

How long does a roof replacement take?

A typical roof replacement in McAllen takes about 1 to 3 days, depending on the size and complexity of the project.

What is the best time of year for roof replacement?

Spring and fall are generally the best times for roof replacement in McAllen due to milder weather conditions, which can facilitate quicker installation.

Will my insurance cover roof replacement?

Many homeowners' insurance policies cover roof replacement if the damage is due to a covered peril, such as a storm. Check with your insurance provider for specifics.

Can I replace my roof myself?

While DIY roof replacement is possible, it requires significant skill and knowledge. Mistakes can lead to costly repairs, so hiring professionals is usually recommended.

What should I do if I notice a leak?

If you notice a leak, it's essential to address it immediately to prevent further damage. Contact a professional for emergency roof repair services.

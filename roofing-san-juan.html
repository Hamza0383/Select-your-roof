San Juan through the year

How Seasons Affect Roofing in San Juan

San Juan experiences distinct seasons that impact roofing demand and maintenance needs. Understanding these seasonal changes can help homeowners prepare effectively.

Spring

Spring brings heavy rains, increasing the likelihood of leaks and water damage. Homeowners should inspect their roofs for potential issues.

Tip: Schedule a roof inspection to address any vulnerabilities before the rainy season peaks.

Summer

Hot temperatures can cause roofing materials to expand and contract, leading to potential cracks.

Tip: Regular maintenance checks during summer can help identify any heat-related damage early.

Fall

Fall often brings storms that can cause debris buildup on roofs, leading to clogging and water retention.

Tip: Consider a thorough cleaning and inspection to prepare your roof for winter.

Winter

While winters are mild, occasional frost can lead to ice damming on roofs, causing leaks.

Tip: Ensure your roof is well-maintained to prevent ice buildup that can damage shingles.

How Seasons Affect Roofing in Plainview

Plainview's climate affects roofing needs throughout the year. Understanding seasonal considerations can help homeowners maintain their roofs effectively.

Spring

Spring brings heavy rains, increasing the risk of leaks and water damage.

Tip: Schedule a roof inspection to identify and address potential issues before the rainy season.

Summer

Summer heat can cause roofing materials to expand and contract, leading to wear.

Tip: Consider routine maintenance to ensure your roof withstands the heat.

Fall

Winter

Tip: Ensure proper insulation and ventilation to prevent ice buildup.

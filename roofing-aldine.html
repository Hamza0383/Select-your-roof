How Seasons Affect Roofing in Aldine

The climate in Aldine significantly influences roofing needs throughout the year. Understanding seasonal impacts can help homeowners prepare.

Spring

Spring often brings heavy rains, increasing the risk of leaks and water damage.

Tip: Schedule a roof inspection after winter to ensure your roof is ready for the rainy season.

Summer

Tip: Regular maintenance in the fall can prevent storm damage and ensure your roof is secure.

Tip: Ensure proper insulation and ventilation to prevent ice buildup and associated damage.

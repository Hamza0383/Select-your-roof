Understanding roof replacement pricing

$5,000 - $15,000 depending on scope

Roof replacement costs in Texas vary based on materials, roof size, and complexity. Typical pricing for 2026 ranges from $5,000 to $15,000. Factors like the choice of asphalt shingles versus metal roofing can significantly affect the overall cost.

Investing in a quality roof replacement protects your home and enhances its value, making it a worthwhile expenditure.

01Roof size

Larger roofs require more materials and labor, increasing the overall cost of replacement.

02Material choice

Different materials have varying costs; for example, metal roofing is typically more expensive than asphalt shingles.

03Labor costs

Labor rates can vary by region; skilled labor may command higher prices but ensure quality installation.

04Roof complexity

Roofs with multiple slopes, valleys, or features require more time and expertise to replace.

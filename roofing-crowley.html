Crowley through the year

How Seasons Affect Roofing in Crowley

Crowley's climate changes throughout the year, impacting roofing needs and maintenance. Understanding these seasonal shifts can help homeowners prepare.

Spring

Spring often brings heavy rains, making it essential to check for leaks and perform maintenance.

Tip: Schedule a roof inspection to ensure your roof can handle the upcoming storms.

Summer

High temperatures can cause roofing materials to expand, leading to potential damage.

Tip: Consider scheduling maintenance to check for heat-related wear and tear.

Fall

Fall brings falling leaves that can clog gutters, leading to water damage if not addressed.

Tip: Regularly clean gutters to prevent water buildup and roof damage.

Winter

Cold temperatures and potential ice can lead to ice damming on roofs, causing leaks.

Tip: Inspect your roof for ice dams and schedule removal before they cause damage.

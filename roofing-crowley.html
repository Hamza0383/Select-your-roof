How Seasons Affect Roofing in Crowley

Crowley's climate plays a significant role in roofing demand throughout the year. Each season presents unique challenges.

Spring

Spring brings heavy rains that can expose roof leaks, making inspections crucial.

Tip: Schedule a roof inspection to catch potential issues before the rainy season.

Summer

Summer heat can cause materials to expand and contract, leading to wear and tear.

Tip: Consider regular maintenance to ensure your roof withstands the summer heat.

Fall

Fall leaves can clog gutters, leading to water pooling and roof damage.

Tip: Clean your gutters regularly to prevent water damage as the leaves fall.

Winter

Winter can bring ice and snow, which can lead to ice damming on roofs.

Tip: Ensure proper insulation to prevent ice dams and protect your roof from winter weather.

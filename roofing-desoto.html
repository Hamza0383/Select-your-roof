How Seasons Affect Roofing in DeSoto

DeSoto's climate significantly influences roofing needs throughout the year. Understanding seasonal impacts can help homeowners prepare accordingly.

Spring

Spring brings heavy rains, which can lead to leaks and water damage if roofs are not properly maintained.

Tip: Schedule a roof inspection to identify any potential issues before the rainy season.

Summer

Hot temperatures can cause roofing materials to expand and contract, leading to cracks or damage.

Tip: Ensure proper ventilation in your attic to prevent heat buildup that can damage your roof.

Fall

Fall is a time for roof maintenance as leaves can clog gutters and cause water buildup.

Tip: Clean gutters regularly to prevent water damage and ensure proper drainage.

Winter

Winter can bring ice and snow, which may lead to ice damming on roofs.

Tip: Consider roof maintenance to prevent ice buildup and ensure proper insulation.

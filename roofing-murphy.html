Murphy through the year

Seasonal Considerations for Roofing in Murphy

Murphy's climate influences roofing needs throughout the year. Understanding seasonal changes can help homeowners prepare for potential issues.

Spring

Spring brings heavy rains, which can lead to leaks if roofs are not properly maintained. It's essential to check for any damage after storms.

Tip: Schedule a roof inspection in early spring to address any potential issues before the rainy season.

Summer

Summer heat can cause shingles to deteriorate and lead to increased energy costs. Regular maintenance is key during this season.

Tip: Consider reflective roofing materials to reduce heat absorption and improve energy efficiency.

Fall

Fall can lead to debris buildup on roofs from falling leaves, which can block gutters and cause water damage.

Tip: Regular cleaning and inspections in the fall can prevent damage from debris and prepare your roof for winter.

Winter

Winter brings the risk of ice dams, which can cause leaks and damage to roofs if not managed properly.

Tip: Ensure proper insulation and ventilation to prevent ice dam formation and protect your roof.

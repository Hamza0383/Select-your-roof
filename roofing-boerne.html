Boerne through the year

How Seasons Affect Roofing in Boerne

Boerne's climate varies throughout the year, impacting roofing needs and maintenance. Understanding seasonal considerations is crucial for homeowners.

Spring

Spring often brings heavy rains that can expose roofing vulnerabilities. Homeowners should inspect for leaks and damage.

Tip: Schedule a roof inspection to identify potential issues before the rainy season begins.

Summer

Summer heat can lead to thermal expansion and contraction of roofing materials, increasing the risk of damage.

Tip: Consider a roof maintenance check to ensure your roof can withstand high temperatures.

Fall

Fall is a great time for roof inspections as leaves can clog gutters, leading to water backup and potential leaks.

Tip: Clean gutters and schedule an inspection to prepare for winter.

Winter

Winter may bring snow and ice, which can add weight to roofs and lead to ice damming.

Tip: Ensure your roof is clear of debris and check for proper insulation to prevent ice buildup.

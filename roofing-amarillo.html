How Seasons Affect Roofing in Amarillo

Seasonal changes in Amarillo bring different challenges for roofing. Understanding these can help homeowners prepare and maintain their roofs effectively.

Spring

Spring often brings heavy rains and potential hail storms, making it crucial to check for leaks and damage.

Tip: Schedule an inspection to address any issues before the storm season peaks.

Summer

Winter

Tip: Ensure your roof has proper insulation and ventilation to prevent ice buildup.

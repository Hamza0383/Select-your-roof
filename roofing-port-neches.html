How Seasons Affect Roofing in Port Neches

The climate in Port Neches significantly impacts roofing needs throughout the year. Understanding seasonal changes can help homeowners prepare.

Spring

Spring brings heavy rains, which can lead to leaks if roofs are not properly maintained.

Summer

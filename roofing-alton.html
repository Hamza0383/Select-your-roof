Alton through the year

How Seasons Affect Roofing in Alton

Alton experiences distinct seasons, each impacting roofing needs and maintenance schedules. Understanding these seasonal changes can help homeowners prepare.

Spring

Spring brings heavy rains, which can lead to roof leaks if not properly maintained. It's crucial to check for any damage after winter.

Tip: Schedule a roof inspection to address any potential issues before the rainy season.

Summer

Summer heat can cause roofing materials to expand and contract, leading to wear and tear over time.

Tip: Ensure proper ventilation to prevent overheating and damage to your roof.

Fall

Fall is the time for falling leaves, which can clog gutters and lead to water pooling on the roof.

Tip: Clean gutters and inspect for any damage before winter sets in.

Winter

Winter brings snow and ice, which can lead to ice damming and additional weight on roofs.

Tip: Consider a roof inspection to ensure your roof can handle winter weather.

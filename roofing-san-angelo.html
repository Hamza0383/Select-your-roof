How Seasons Affect Roofing in San Angelo

San Angelo's climate influences roofing needs throughout the year. Understanding these seasonal changes can help homeowners prepare.

Spring

Spring often brings heavy rains, increasing the risk of leaks and water damage.

Tip: Schedule a roof inspection to address any potential leaks before the rainy season.

Summer

Summer heat can cause shingles to deteriorate faster, leading to potential leaks.

Tip: Regular maintenance checks are essential to ensure your roof can withstand the summer heat.

Fall

Fall leaves can clog gutters, leading to water pooling on roofs and causing damage.

Tip: Clean gutters regularly to prevent blockages and ensure proper drainage.

Winter

Winter can bring ice and snow, leading to ice damming and leaks.

Tip: Ensure your roof is well-insulated to prevent ice damming and protect against leaks.

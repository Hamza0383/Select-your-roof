How Seasons Affect Roofing in Kerrville

Kerrville's climate varies throughout the year, impacting roofing needs and maintenance. Understanding these seasonal changes is crucial for homeowners.

Spring

Spring brings heavy rains and potential hailstorms, leading to increased roofing issues.

Tip: Schedule a roof inspection after storms to assess any damage.

Summer

Summer heat can cause roofing materials to expand and contract, leading to wear and tear.

Tip: Consider a roof maintenance check to ensure your roof can withstand the heat.

Fall

Fall is the ideal time for roof inspections as leaves can clog gutters and lead to water damage.

Tip: Clean gutters and schedule an inspection before winter weather sets in.

Winter

Winter can bring ice and snow, which may lead to ice damming and roof leaks.

Tip: Ensure your roof is clear of debris and check for potential leaks before winter storms.

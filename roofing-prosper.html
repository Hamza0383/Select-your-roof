How Seasons Affect Roofing in Prosper

The climate in Prosper influences roofing needs throughout the year. Understanding these seasonal changes can help homeowners prepare.

Spring

Spring brings heavy rains that can expose roof vulnerabilities. Regular inspections are essential during this season.

Tip: Schedule a roof inspection in early spring to address any winter damage before the rainy season.

Summer

Tip: Ensure your roof is well-insulated to prevent ice dams and schedule inspections before winter hits.

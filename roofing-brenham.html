How Seasons Affect Roofing in Brenham

Brenham's seasonal changes significantly influence roofing demands. Understanding these patterns helps homeowners prepare for potential issues throughout the year.

Spring

Spring brings heavy rains, which can lead to roof leaks and water damage if not properly maintained.

Tip: Schedule a roof inspection in early spring to address any potential issues before the rainy season.

Summer

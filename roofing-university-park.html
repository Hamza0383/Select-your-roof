How Seasons Affect Roofing in University Park

The climate in University Park influences roofing demands throughout the year. Understanding seasonal challenges can help homeowners prepare.

Spring

Spring often brings heavy rains, which can expose leaks and damage.

Tip: Schedule a roof inspection to identify and address potential issues before the rainy season.

Summer

Intense heat can cause roofing materials to expand and contract, leading to wear.

Tip: Consider reflective roofing materials to reduce heat absorption and improve energy efficiency.

Fall

Fall is the time for falling leaves, which can clog gutters and lead to water pooling on roofs.

Tip: Regular maintenance, including gutter cleaning, is essential to prevent water damage.

Winter

Snow and ice can accumulate on roofs, leading to potential leaks and ice dams.

Tip: Ensure proper insulation and ventilation to minimize ice dam formation.

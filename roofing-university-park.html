Seasonal Considerations for Roofing in University Park

The climate in University Park affects roofing demands throughout the year. Understanding seasonal challenges can help homeowners prepare.

Spring

Spring brings heavy rains, increasing the risk of leaks and water damage.

Tip: Schedule a roof inspection to ensure your roof can handle the spring storms.

Summer

Summer heat can cause roofing materials to expand and contract, leading to wear.

Tip: Consider a maintenance check to address any potential issues before the heat peaks.

Fall

Fall leaves can clog gutters and downspouts, leading to drainage issues.

Tip: Regular gutter cleaning is essential to prevent water pooling on your roof.

Winter

Winter can bring ice damming, which can cause leaks and damage to the roof structure.

Tip: Ensure your roof is insulated properly to minimize the risk of ice damming.

How Seasons Affect Roofing in Galena Park

Galena Park experiences distinct seasons, each impacting roofing needs. Understanding these seasonal considerations can help homeowners maintain their roofs effectively.

Spring

Spring brings heavy rains, increasing the risk of leaks. Homeowners should inspect roofs for any damage after storms.
